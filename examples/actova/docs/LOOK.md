# LOOK: Actova launch film

## Canvas
- Background: warm white #F6F5F1 (the product's own canvas). No dark scenes.
- Focus tint: a soft #FFEDE5 (accent-soft) wash behind whatever is in focus

## Type
- Headlines: Inter 800, letter-spacing -0.045em, ink #15171C
- Accent word: Instrument Serif italic, accent #FF5A1F, one per headline ("didn't", "finish", "12 seconds")
- UI / captions: Inter 600
- Minimum on-screen size: 28 px at full resolution

## Color
- Accent #FF5A1F: Actova acting (recording, highlighting, creating)
- Good #1E8E5A: done / sent only
- Owner colors only on avatars

## UI presentation
- The product's real components (same markup and CSS as the app), on white cards:
  radius 18, 1px #E7E5DF border, shadow 0 12px 32px rgba(21,23,28,.05)
- Cursor: black arrow, scales with the camera

## Motion grammar
- Text rises from a mask line, one word per beat. Nothing fades.
- Handoffs through one orange dot (the record light): the film starts on it, every scene
  hands over through a dot, pill or card, and the film ends on it (loop).
- Highlights sweep left to right across each promise, one per beat.
- Springs: snappy on UI and the cursor, heavy on headlines, never playful.
- Camera: push in on the working area, one move at a time, log-space zoom.

## Sound
- Music: minimal, upbeat, 110-125 BPM, clear drop (placeholder until the user's track)
- Effects: pop = promise caught · click = cursor · whoosh = big move · ding = recap sent

## Banned for this film
- Everything in house-rules §4, plus: third-party logos, fake notification spam, emoji.
