# Video brief: Actova launch film

## The film
- Product / subject: Actova, an AI meeting assistant. **Fictional product for the Motion Studio case study.**
- URL (or the pages / screenshots to use): the Actova app (`../actova/index.html`), 5 screens + states:
  meetings, meeting (live / done), tasks (empty / full), recap (draft / sent), week
- What it does, in 5 lines: Listens to a meeting. Catches every promise people make
  ("I'll send the deck by Friday"). Turns each promise into a task with an owner and a
  due date. Sends the team a recap in one click. Shows the time saved each week.
- The pain it removes (the hook, in words): "The meeting ended. The work didn't start."
- 3 features to show: live transcript with promises highlighted · tasks created with
  owners and dates · one-click recap
- The payoff moment: 45-minute meeting → 9 tasks in 12 seconds. Second beat: "5h 20m back this week".
- Call to action: "Meetings that finish themselves." + wordmark (no URL: fictional)

## Format
- Duration: 30 s (60 beats at 120 BPM)
- Formats: 9:16 vertical (primary) and 16:9 wide
- Will it loop? Yes
- Where it will be posted: case study (GitHub README, X, LinkedIn)
